What it is

A team runtime. Not a framework.

  • Bring the agents you already use. We make them one team.
  • Objective → allocate → concurrent → handoffs → review → replan.
  • Not a multi-agent framework, a model router, or a shared-memory product.

How

Objective

Standing mission. Met, not met, or evidence. No invented bars.

Team

Seats and coverage. Empty seats are uncovered work.

Work

Teammates, lanes, next step, handoff. Blocked · Ready · In flight · Review.

Intervene

Route, unblock, reassign, nudge, msg. No silent auto-promote.

Metrics

efficiency score · unmeasured

Not measured until real. The score is turns to done. Unknown is not zero. No sample numbers.
