Open the repository How it runs

BYOA

Bring your own agent harness — Claude, Codex, Cursor, a local model, a shell one-liner. We do not install a model. The runtime makes them one team: objective, seats, handoffs, review.

Usage-aware

Turns and cost count when the harness reports them. Unknown is not zero. The point is yield: more work finished, fewer turns, least spend.

Total Football

Positions are coverage, not identity. Any seat can take any lane — including master. Empty seats are uncovered work. The operator intervenes; the product does not silently auto-promote.

Open first. You host it.

Atman is who works and how the team finishes. The CLI is how it runs today. The product is the team runtime — not a hosted dashboard you subscribe to.

  • Open repository. MIT licensed. You clone it.
  • Self-hosted on your machine or your filesystem. No Atman cloud.
  • Not Steer. Steer is a separate policy product. Atman does not enforce what an agent may see or say.
